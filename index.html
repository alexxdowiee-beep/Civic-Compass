How to vote

The practical steps, key dates, and the terms you'll see everywhere but nobody explains.

Key dates
25
Oct
Enrolment closes at midnight
This is a genuine change for 2026 — in past elections you could enrol right up to and including election day. Not this time. If you're not enrolled (or your enrolled address is out of date) by midnight on this day, you can't vote. Check or update in under a minute at vote.nz.
26
Oct
Advance voting opens
You can vote any day from here through to the day before election day — no need to wait for 7 Nov, and no reason given required. Advance voting places are usually open longer hours and are less busy than election day.
7
Nov
Election Day
Voting places are open 9am–7pm. If you're enrolled, you can vote at any voting place in the country, not just ones in your own electorate — though it may take a little longer if you're outside your electorate, since your vote is processed as a "special vote."
Am I eligible?
  • You're 18 or older on election day
  • You're a NZ citizen, or a permanent resident who's lived here continuously for at least 12 months at some point
  • You're enrolled by the 25 October deadline above
What to bring / what happens
  • If you've enrolled and it's close to the election, you'll get an EasyVote card in the post or by email — bring it if you have it, it speeds things up, but it's not required
  • No card? You'll just be asked your name and address — it may take a bit longer while staff find you on the roll
  • You'll get two votes: one for your local electorate MP, one for your party vote — see the glossary below for what the difference actually means
